Cuisine

Each American state has its own distinct cuisine shaped by its unique local geography, historical immigration patterns, and indigenous ingredients. For instance, coastal Maine’s identity is tied to abundant local lobster, whereas New Mexico’s food culture centers around native Hatch green chiles and Spanish-Pueblo roots. These localized traditions turn individual states into distinct culinary micro-regions, rather than just parts of a single national flavor.
