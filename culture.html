Fun Fact!

The 50-star American flag we see today was actually designed by a 17-year-old high school student named Bob Heft in 1958 as a school history project. He originally got a B-minus on the project from his teacher, who thought lack of imagination was an issue—but after President Eisenhower picked Heft's design out of thousands submitted, the teacher agreed to change his grade to an A!

Fun Fact!

Ever notice how so many US holidays always land on a Monday? That is by design! Thanks to the Uniform Monday Holiday Act of 1968, federal holidays like Memorial Day, Presidents' Day, and Labor Day were intentionally shifted to Mondays to ensure workers got a guaranteed three-day weekend.

Fun Fact!

The United States has a fascinating legal system because each of the 50 states can pass its own legislation. Over the centuries, this has left some incredibly specific, outdated, and downright hilarious archaic laws still on the books that nobody has ever bothered to officially repeal.
