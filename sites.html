Utah

Utah features stunning natural landscapes, ranging from the dramatic red rock arches of the south to the snowy peaks of the north. In Moab, visitors can explore Arches National Park and Canyonlands National Park, famous for their massive sandstone arches and deep canyons carved by rivers.

Oregon

Oregon has amazing, straightforward spots to visit that don't require complicated planning. Whether you want crashing ocean waves or towering waterfalls, here are some of the easiest and most rewarding places to check out.

Minnesota

If you love the outdoors, Itasca State Park is a classic stop where you can walk across the shallow, trickling start of the Mississippi River. Up along Lake Superior, the rugged North Shore features the historic Split Rock Lighthouse perched high on a dramatic rocky cliff. For a true wilderness escape, the Boundary Waters Canoe Area Wilderness offers quiet lakes and pine forests for peaceful paddling and camping.

Texas

Texas has three simple destinations to visit: Austin, San Antonio, and Big Bend. In Austin, you can hear live music and eat great barbecue. In San Antonio, you can walk the River Walk and see the Alamo. At Big Bend, you can hike desert trails and look at the stars.

Washington D.C.

D.C. is incredibly easy to visit because the famous monuments and massive museums sit right next to each other on a flat, walkable strip. Best of all, almost every major sight is completely free and requires zero advanced planning. If you want a break from the crowds, you can take a relaxing stroll along the Georgetown waterfront or around the Tidal Basin.
